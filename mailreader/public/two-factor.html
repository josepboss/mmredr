About this tool

Generates standard six-digit, SHA-1 time-based verification codes (TOTP), refreshed every 30 seconds. Use the Base32 setup key supplied by your account provider. Keep your device’s date and time set automatically so codes match.

Treat your secret key like a password. Only enter keys for accounts you own or are authorized to access. This tool does not store your keys or provide account recovery.
